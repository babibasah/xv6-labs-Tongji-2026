1. Which registers contain arguments to functions? For example, which register holds 13 in main's call to printf?
Function arguments are passed in registers a0-a7.
Register a2 holds the value 13 in main's call to printf.

2. Where is the call to function f in the assembly code for main? Where is the call to g?
There are no calls to f or g in main. The compiler inlined both functions and precomputed the expression f(8)+1 to the constant 12 at compile time.
